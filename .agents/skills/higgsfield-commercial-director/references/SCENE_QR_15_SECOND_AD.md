# Scene QR — 15-second concept: 'SCAN. REQUEST. DONE.'

## Promise
Customers scan a business's QR, ask for help or a quote, and the staff gets a contextual request. Show the handoff, not unexplained automation.

## Shot plan — 9:16
| Time | Visual and action | Camera | Editorial text (add after rendering) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–2.5s | Restaurant customer looks for help, spots Scene QR tabletop card; phone enters frame | Eye-level 35mm fast push to QR | 'Customers need help.' |
| 2.5–5.5s | Same customer scans and taps simple 'Need assistance' request; server sees a table request and turns toward them | Tight over-shoulder 50mm match cut | 'Scan → Request' |
| 5.5–9.0s | Auto shop guest scans QR at desk and asks for a quote; adviser receives request and acknowledges | Close-medium 35mm handheld-stable | 'Quotes, too.' |
| 9.0–12.5s | Store shopper scans product shelf QR, asks a product question; associate approaches the shopper | Clean tracking move, 35mm | 'And customer questions.' |
| 12.5–15.0s | Crisp brand card over a real QR stand with owner/staff helping someone, final frame held | Locked 50mm | 'SCENE QR — Put every scan to work.' + accurate site URL |

## Model-facing single-generation prompt (creative direction, no credits spent)
Photorealistic 15-second vertical small-business TV commercial, quick precise cuts across a restaurant, auto service desk, and retail shop. At each location, an adult customer scans a physical QR card with a correctly sized smartphone, taps one simple request, and an employee notices a notification and responds in person. Three distinct locations and casts; no morph transitions. Natural human timing and clear eye lines, physically believable fingers and props. Restrained graphite and ivory art direction, warm practical lighting, clean 35mm over-shoulder shots and motivated push-ins. Strong opening action in the first second. Finish with a stable two-second Scene QR brand plate for editorial text. Polished, credible, energetic commercial pacing.

## Critical note on output reliability
This is intentionally difficult for a one-pass generation: three industries + phone UI + accurate request handoffs in 15 seconds. Best result is normally achieved by preparing *actual product screenshot overlays* and the brand/URL card for editing. If the trial permits only one generation, review the selected model's ability to handle cuts, text and exact UI before committing. A simpler one-industry demonstration may convert better and render more reliably.

## Suggested overlay / sound plan
- Use real Scene QR screenshots or animations inserted on the phone screen in editing; do not rely on generated text.
- Foley: scan beep at 2.5s, gentle notification at the server/adviser/associate response.
- Optional VO (short and natural): 'They scan. You get the request. Your team handles it. Scene QR.'
- Last frame and overlays must use verified CTA URL, not hallucinated AI lettering.
- If the product is not yet publicly ready for signup, use 'Request a demo' rather than 'Sign up now'.
