# Moderated guest + staff test

Suggested length: 10–15 minutes per pair; scheduling is [TO CONFIRM].
Station and request scenario are [TO CONFIRM]. Use the correct business's natural
setting: restaurant table, barber chair, service bay or shop counter.

## Opening

“We’re testing the product, not you. Please say what you expect as you go.
There are no right answers. You can stop at any point. Please use the test
information provided rather than personal details.”

Explain any recording separately and get consent before starting. Do not promise
anonymity if the chosen recording process could identify someone.

## 1. First impression — guest

Show the published page without explaining Scene.
- “What do you think this is for?”
- “What would you expect to happen after scanning this code?”
- “Who do you think sees a request?”

Record the participant's own words. Do not score by whether they repeat branding.

## 2. Request help — guest

“You’re at this [table/chair/bay/counter] and need [approved test assistance].
Use what’s available here to ask for help.”

Let them discover the QR and action. Observe whether they can scan/open it,
identify the business/station, choose help and understand the send result. Note
if they think they must install an app, register, pay or re-enter their location.
If they get stuck, ask “What are you looking for?” before helping. Record exactly
what assistance was provided; do not silently turn an assisted attempt into a pass.

## 3. Receive and act — staff

Provide the approved test queue/account without describing where station labels are.
“A guest has asked for help. Find their request and tell me what you would do next.”

Observe: correct business; request type/message; ability to state the correct
station; status control; ability to mark in progress, then completed. Do not prompt
“look for the location badge” before their first attempt.

## 4. Follow completion — guest

Return to the guest's original receipt.
“What do you think is happening with your request now?”

Observe whether new/in progress/completed is understandable and updates. Measure
only observed time. Never call a request paid, a guaranteed arrival time or a
confirmed reservation. Record whether completion is mistaken for staff physically
arriving; status alone is not proof that service was delivered.

## 5. Debrief

Use survey.md. Separate what people said from what you observed. Do not argue with
confusion or sell them the intended benefit. Thank them without claiming they
endorsed Scene. Only offer incentives if separately approved; none are promised.
