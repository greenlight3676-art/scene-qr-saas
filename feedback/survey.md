# Post-task questions

Participant alias: [TO CONFIRM] · Guest/staff: [TO CONFIRM]
Device/browser: [TO CONFIRM] · Session date: [TO CONFIRM]

Ask only after observing the task:

1. In your own words, what did Scene let you do?
2. Which part, if any, was confusing?
3. What did you expect after sending or receiving the request?
4. Guest: how did you know which location staff would see?
   Staff: how did you decide where to go?
5. How did you know whether the request was still waiting or completed?
6. How easy or difficult was this task? Very difficult / difficult / neither /
   easy / very easy. What made you choose that answer?
7. How would you normally handle this situation? Would Scene change that?
8. What would make you choose not to use it?
9. What is the one change you would make first?

Optional follow-up research contact requires separate participant permission.
Leave missing answers blank. Do not treat preference answers as observed task
completion or convert this small round into a product-wide customer satisfaction
score. Store real responses privately, not in this repository.
